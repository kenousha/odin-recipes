Lasagna

Description

A very easy recipe for lasagna

Ingredients

  • Onion (frozen & pre-chopped)
  • Minced beef
  • Frozen vegetable medley
  • Tomato Sauce
  • White sauce
  • Lasagne sheets (dry not fresh)

Steps

  1. Soften the onions and vegetable medley in a large saucepan with a little oil.
  2. Add the minced beef to the pan and cook until browned.
  3. First mix the tomato sauce mixture with the mince mixture.
  4. Then add a thin layer of your beef and tomato mixture to the bottom of the dish.
  5. Then add a small thin layer of the white sauce, and then add your sheets of whole-wheat pasta.
  6. Layer like this until you reach the top of the dish, finishing off with a layer of white sauce.
  7. Cook at180c for 30-40 minutes.
